Two roasters and a very old oven.

Lumen started in a garage in 2016 and moved into a proper shop in 2019.

Our story

We wanted coffee that tasted like the place it came from, so we learned to roast lightly and brew carefully.

Today the team is eleven people, one dog, and a roaster we have named Gerald.

What we believe

Slow is fine

Good coffee takes the time it takes.

Be specific

Name the farm, the lot and the date.

Stay small

One shop, done well.
