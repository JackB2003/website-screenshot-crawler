Why we roast light

Flavor, not smoke.

Roasting is a conversation with the bean. Push too hard and you only hear yourself.

A lighter roast keeps fruit, florals and sweetness that dark roasting burns away.

If you prefer something deeper, ask. We will happily pull a darker batch for you.
